About us

DAG International Ltd builds systems that are tailor-fit to the size, budget and needs of each customer. Our customers are empowered by our technical expertise, and we deliver customized solutions on time and on budget.

The key to our success is a close working relationship with clients. We learn their objectives so every solution helps them get the most from it. Our backbone is professionals with wide experience in communications and information technology, led by young qualified engineers.

To establish ourselves as the best choice in computing and information technology services by offering the full spectrum of services.

Vision

To become an entity in technology-based corporate solutions, capable of earning a response from the niche we serve.

Purpose

To achieve customer satisfaction by delivering quality products and services at an affordable cost and in a timely manner.
