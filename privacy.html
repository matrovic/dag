Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

DAG International Ltd respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect through this website and our services, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Details you give us: your name, phone number, email address and the message you send through our contact form, email, phone or WhatsApp.
  • Project information you share so we can do the work, such as business details and content for your website or app.

How we use it

We use your information to reply to you, prepare quotes, deliver and support our services, send invoices, and meet legal duties. We do not sell your personal information.

Payments

Card payments are handled by Flutterwave, a licensed payment provider. Your card details go directly to Flutterwave and are never stored on our website or systems.

We share information only with service providers who help us deliver your project (for example hosting or payment providers), or when the law requires it. We keep your information only as long as needed for these purposes and protect it with reasonable access controls.
